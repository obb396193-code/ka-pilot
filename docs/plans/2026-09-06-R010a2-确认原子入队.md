> **For Codex:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** 按P006把confirm/retry成功状态、pending实际execution_run和持久化job在同事务提交。

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript、pg、现有JobRepository、Vitest。
